## Dataset licence scope

The published dataset uses Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), only where the dataset publishers have authority to license the material. The Hugging Face Dataset Card defines the released files, attribution, and exclusions.

Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Scope

The root MIT LICENSE applies to the PathFACT software, not automatically to separately distributed datasets.

Dataset licensing applies only to the materials identified in the published v0.1 release and its Dataset Card.

Third-party materials, external model weights, and content subject to separate terms are not automatically covered.

LLM-generated responses may not qualify for copyright protection, but applicable provider terms and other restrictions must still be respected.
